# Product detail page

`/products` and `/products/ceramic-tile-fix` display the first product detail page. The Products mega menu's Standard & Polymer Adhesives link opens this local page.

The page includes a three-view gallery with an accessible image dialog, manufacturer TDS link, SDS request, 20/25 kg and pallet order choices, quantity controls, keyboard-accessible technical tabs, a coverage estimator, complementary system links and a prefilled BOQ/quote form.

## Confirmed product information

The first product is the actual [Vetonit Ceramic Tile Fix](https://www.saveto.com/products/ceramic-tile-fix). Its manufacturer description supports internal ceramic/terrazzo installations; the wireframe's porcelain, C1T, strength, temperature and VOC examples are not assumed to apply. Mixing quantities, working time and application steps are drawn from the manufacturer page. The current TDS remains the authority for the supplied product variant.

TDS access uses the manufacturer's account-gated download destination. SDS is requested through the inquiry form until an approved file is supplied.

## Planning and commercial details

- No invented SKU, warehouse location, live stock badge, prices or volume discounts are displayed.
- Default pallet conversion of 60 bags is editable and marked as a planning assumption.
- Calculator rates (2.5/3.5/5 kg per square meter), notch presets and waste allowance are editable estimates, not published product coverage. Results round up bags and pallets.
- Estimator input is bounded; invalid values disable quote actions. Selected quantities/units and document requests populate the embedded inquiry form.
- Sales callback requests are available while verified WhatsApp and phone details are pending.
- Quote delivery still requires the configured server receiver described in HOMEPAGE-SETUP.md.

Gallery uses the manufacturer's existing product bag image, an enlarged front detail, and the official tiling application photo. It does not fabricate a back-label image. Complementary card images are marked as representative material-family packaging.
